Is it a lot of red? Absolutely. Do I have any regrets? Absolutely
not. The stripes and the florals only get along because they agreed
on a color first; keep two prints in the same palette and the whole
outfit reads as one intentional idea.

The brooch is the quiet hero here. Pinned at the top button, it does
what a necklace would, but it makes the shirt look custom instead of
accessorized. Now excuse me while I answer emails dressed like I have
my life completely together.
